# 03 — Users

## Primary
- Operators and founders coordinating messy projects.
- Professional services teams reconstructing client cases.
- Engineers receiving fragmented requirements.
- Researchers synthesizing evidence and unresolved questions.

## Judge persona
A hackathon judge should understand MAAT in under 30 seconds: paste a messy situation, press **BUILD CASE**, then watch the system produce a graph and defensible next action.
