## Primary interaction
One dominant CTA: **BUILD CASE**.

## Judge-visible sequence
1. Paste messy context.
2. Nodes materialize by type.
3. Edges reveal dependencies.
4. Risk/evidence-gap panel updates.
5. Next-best action appears with rationale.
6. Proof receipt becomes downloadable/copyable.

## Design rule
The graph is the explanation. Avoid burying agency inside chat transcripts.
