# 13 — Proof

## Evidence classes
- **SOURCE** — original user/input text.
- **DERIVED** — structured node/edge inferred from source.
- **EXECUTION** — tool/action result.
- **VERIFICATION** — test or check against a claimed result.
- **RECEIPT** — immutable summary connecting claim to evidence.

## Promotion law
No result advances to VERIFIED based solely on model prose. Verification requires a deterministic check or explicit human acceptance recorded in the receipt.
