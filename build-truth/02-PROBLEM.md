# 02 — Problem

Professional work arrives fragmented across prose, messages, notes, files, changing requirements, deadlines, people, and partial evidence. Humans spend substantial effort reconstructing state before they can act.

MAAT addresses the state-reconstruction bottleneck by making actors, claims, evidence, requirements, events, risks, tasks, dependencies, contradictions, and decisions first-class graph objects.

## Failure to solve
Without explicit state, teams duplicate work, act on stale assumptions, miss blockers, and cannot explain why a decision was made.
